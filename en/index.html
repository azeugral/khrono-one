Questions

Before you start.

What is the difference between Basic and Pro?
Basic covers 1 business and includes the service catalogue, client management, team management, calendar and public booking. Pro covers up to 3 businesses and adds finances and payments, team permissions and the loyalty programme.
Do my clients need an account?
No. They book through the business link without an account. Khrono also offers a client area to follow bookings and, on Pro, loyalty.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. Khrono runs in the browser and adapts to phone and desktop, with nothing to install.
How do I start the 7 free days?
Click “Start free” and create the account with your name, e-mail and business name. If you want to carry on after seven days, you pick a plan from the dashboard.
I already have an account. Where do I sign in?
Use “Sign in” at the top of the page. Your clients keep using your business booking link and the client area.
